Workout Trimmer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Developer: Bob Meyer

Workout Trimmer is an iPhone app that helps you trim workouts that kept recording after you stopped (for example after a run when you drove home). It reads and writes health and fitness data on your device through Apple Health (HealthKit).

Data we access

  • Workouts and workout routes (GPS) — to show your recent workouts and detect a likely end time.
  • Heart rate, steps, energy burned, distance, and related workout samples — to display charts and to copy into a new trimmed workout when you ask.

The app only uses this data on your device. It does not upload your health data to our servers. There is no account and no analytics SDK in this version.

What we write

When you tap Save trimmed workout to Health, the app creates a new workout in Apple Health with the trimmed time range and the samples HealthKit allows a third-party app to write. It does not delete the original workout (Apple only lets an app delete workouts it created). You choose whether to delete the original in the Fitness or Health app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your health data. Debug “Export samples” (if shown) shares a JSON file you choose via the system share sheet; that stays under your control.

Permissions

iOS asks you to allow Health access. You can change or revoke it anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Workout Trimmer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
